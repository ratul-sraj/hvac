    3. Draw rooms on the plan

    Set the drawing scale, then use Draw shape to add a room — one tool, two gestures. Drag over a room for a plain rectangle: the area is measured from the rectangle and that scale, and it becomes a new room straight away. Click each corner to trace a room with any number of sides: click the first corner again (or double-click, or press Enter) to close it, and choose which room the shape belongs to — a new room, or an existing row whose area the shape then sets. The boxes below are the rooms you have placed; rooms found by reading the PDF stay in the table, but Place all rooms on the plan gives every one the drawing names a locator box where the sheet names it, sized back from that room's own area (it is a locator, not the traced room boundary). Trace real outlines reads the plan's own wall lines and gives the rooms it can trust a real outline shape — a room whose shape the plan does not enclose, or whose traced area does not agree with the stated one, keeps its box. Remove outlines takes those shapes away again and leaves the boxes alone. A drawing that prints room names but no areas: Fill areas from the drawing measures an area from the plan's own outline, but only when exactly one room name sits inside one closed shape — it says what it filled and what it left alone, and Undo fill puts it back. Remove placed rooms takes those boxes away again and never touches the rooms you drew yourself. Click a box in Select / edit to open that room's breakdown. In Select / edit you can also drag a room to move it, drag a corner to resize it, press Delete to remove it, and middle-drag or hold Space to pan the sheet. All of this stays in your browser, and the drawing is kept here too — reload the page, or come back tomorrow, and it is still here with your boxes on it.
